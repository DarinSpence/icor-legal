  1. Who this applies to. ICOR Reporting ("the App") is an internal reporting tool operated by In:trench Consulting Group ("In:trench"). It is not offered to the public. Only In:trench and the businesses it manages, with their authorization, may use it.
  2. What the App does. The App connects to QuickBooks Online through Intuit's API to read financial reports (such as Profit & Loss, Balance Sheet, General Ledger and Accounts Receivable aging). In:trench uses them to prepare management and board reports for the connected company.
