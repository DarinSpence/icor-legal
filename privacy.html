ICOR Reporting – Privacy Policy

Effective October 23, 2026

  1. Scope. This policy covers ICOR Reporting ("the App"), an internal reporting tool operated by In:trench Consulting Group ("In:trench") for the businesses it manages.
  2. Data we access. With a company administrator's authorization, the App reads accounting data from QuickBooks Online: financial reports, account and class lists, and company information. It does not access payment card or bank login data, and it does not write to QuickBooks.
  3. How we use it. Solely to prepare financial analysis and board reports for the connected company and its owners.
  4. Where it is stored. Report data is stored on In:trench-controlled devices and in the connected company's own document storage. Access credentials are stored only on In:trench devices, readable only by the account that runs the App, and are never shared.
  5. Sharing. We do not sell or share the data with third parties. Reports go only to the connected company's owners, management and advisors it designates (such as its CPA).
  6. Retention and deletion. Data is kept only as long as needed for reporting. A company can disconnect the App at any time in QuickBooks Online (Settings > Apps) and request deletion of retained data by email.
  7. Contact. Darin Spence, In:trench Consulting Group – darin@in-trench.com
